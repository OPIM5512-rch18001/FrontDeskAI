# Club Studio South Windsor - Booking and Class Policies

## How members book classes at Club Studio

Members book classes in the Club Studio mobile app (iPhone or Android) or on the website. The app account is set up with the member barcode; members missing a barcode can email info@clubstudiofitness.com.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## When booking opens for a Club Studio class

A class opens for booking one week ahead, one hour after its start time. Example: a 10:00 AM class next Saturday opens for booking at 11:00 AM this Saturday. Standard Plus and Premier members with Priority Booking can reserve up to 10 days ahead.
Source: website FAQ and rate page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Late cancellation and no-show fees at Club Studio

Cancel at least 12 hours before class to avoid a fee. Cancelling later than that costs $10 as a late cancellation, and not showing up costs $15 as a no-show. Members who book but don't check in at the front desk can also be charged the no-show fee.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Waitlists for full classes at Club Studio

If a class is full, members can join the waitlist. When a spot opens, waitlisted members are enrolled automatically and notified by app and email. Late-cancel and no-show fees still apply once enrolled from the waitlist, so members should take themselves off the waitlist if they can no longer go.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Check-in and arrival at Club Studio classes

Scan in at the front desk before class. Arrive 10 minutes early, or 15 minutes early for a first visit. Doors close when class starts, so latecomers may not be let in.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Class booking transfers and class etiquette at Club Studio

A class booking cannot be shared with or transferred to another person. Phones should be off or on do-not-disturb during class, and selfies are allowed only of yourself.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## What to bring to each Club Studio class

- Reform: grip socks are required.
- Hot Pilates and Hot Pilates X Strength: grip socks encouraged.
- Box classes: boxing gloves required, available to rent for $5; hand wraps strongly recommended.
- Ride: SPD or Delta cleats work on the bikes, or wear regular sneakers.
- Heated Sweat+ classes: water and a towel are a good idea; towel service is available at the club. Whether yoga mats are provided is not published.
Source: website FAQ and club page. Verified 2026-11-06.
