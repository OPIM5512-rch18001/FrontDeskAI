# Club Studio South Windsor - Club Basics

## What Club Studio is

Club Studio is a boutique-style fitness brand that combines studio classes, gym equipment, and recovery and wellness services in one club. It is operated by Fitness International, the company behind LA Fitness. The South Windsor club offers five studios plus specialty classes: Reform (reformer Pilates), CS4 (circuit training), Ride (indoor cycling), Box (boxing and strength), and Sweat+ (heated yoga and Pilates).
Source: homepage and South Windsor club page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Amenities confirmed at Club Studio South Windsor

The South Windsor club page lists: free weights, cardio and strength equipment, locker rooms, a basketball court, an indoor pool, a sauna, a functional training area, a recovery room, cryotherapy, red light therapy, personal training, towel service, Wi-Fi, and a juice bar called Beaming Cafe. Some recovery services cost extra on top of membership.
Source: South Windsor club page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Amenities NOT confirmed at Club Studio South Windsor

The South Windsor amenity list does not include a steam room, spa or whirlpool, racquetball or squash courts, or pickleball courts. Brand-wide marketing mentions these at select clubs only.
Source: membership rate page versus South Windsor club page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Other Club Studio locations in Connecticut

As of November 2026, South Windsor is the only open Club Studio in Connecticut. Two more are listed as coming soon: Norwalk at 761 Main Avenue, and Stamford at Harbor Point, 699 Canal Street.
