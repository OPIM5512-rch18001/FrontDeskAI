# Club Studio South Windsor - Memberships and Pricing

## Membership options and monthly prices at Club Studio South Windsor

Advertised online rates for South Windsor as of November 6, 2026, before tax:
- Standard: $169 per month. Access to the South Windsor club.
- Standard Plus: $209 per month. Adds Priority Booking and 25 guest passes per year.
- Premier: $249 per month. Access to all Club Studio locations in every state, plus Priority Booking and 25 guest passes per year.
All three are month-to-month and auto-renew. The online page shows no initiation fee and no annual fee. Prices can change without notice, and tax, promo codes, and in-club offers are not confirmed.
Source: South Windsor online enrollment rate page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Upfront cost to join Club Studio South Windsor

When joining online, the first payment covers the first month and the last month of dues. That is $338 for Standard, $418 for Standard Plus, and $498 for Premier, plus any tax. The prepaid last month is used after a member cancels.
Source: South Windsor online enrollment rate page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Group and family pricing at Club Studio South Windsor

The Standard membership shows multi-person pricing: 2 people for $318 per month, 3 for $467, and 4 for $616. Multi-person pricing for Standard Plus and Premier is not shown.
Source: South Windsor online enrollment rate page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## What unlimited classes means at Club Studio

Memberships include boutique classes. Unlimited means up to five different boutique classes per day, subject to class size and availability. Online booking allows one reserved spot per studio per day; additional classes in the same studio that day are walk-in only if space allows.
Source: rate page footnotes. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Priority Booking at Club Studio

Standard Plus and Premier members get Priority Booking: they can reserve one spot per studio per day up to 10 days in advance, instead of the standard 7 days. It does not guarantee a spot, because popular classes can still fill.
Source: rate page footnotes. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Recovery add-ons and personal training at Club Studio

Recovery packages are available at an extra monthly cost. The Recovery package includes HydroMassage beds, Normatec compression, Hyperice tools, and a Club VIP card for partner discounts. A higher package adds red light therapy and cryotherapy. Prices for these add-ons and for personal training are not published online.
Source: South Windsor online enrollment rate page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Payment rules and age requirements at Club Studio

Monthly dues are paid by automatic electronic transfer from a checking or savings account, or by an accepted credit card. Members must be at least 16, or 13 with a legal guardian; a guardian 18 or older must buy and sign for a minor.
Source: website legal footer. Verified 2026-11-06.
