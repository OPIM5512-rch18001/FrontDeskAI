# Club Studio South Windsor - Classes and Studios

## Reform (reformer Pilates) at Club Studio South Windsor

Reform is a 45-minute, low-impact reformer Pilates class on custom reformer machines. It focuses on form and time under tension and is open to all levels. Grip socks are required. Callers may call it reformer, reformer Pilates, or the Pilates machine class. Reform is the most frequently scheduled class at South Windsor and tends to fill up first.
Source: website FAQ and Studios page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Pilates X Strength and Hot Pilates X Strength at Club Studio South Windsor

Pilates X Strength combines Pilates with strength work using dumbbells and bands. Hot Pilates X Strength is the same idea in a heated room of roughly 90 degrees Fahrenheit. Grip socks are encouraged. Exact class length is not published; plan on about 45 minutes.
Source: Club Studio schedule descriptions. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Hot Pilates at Club Studio South Windsor

Hot Pilates is a 45-minute mat Pilates class with barre and dance influences, in a room heated up to about 85 degrees Fahrenheit. Grip socks are encouraged. It is low impact and suitable for beginners who are comfortable with heat.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## CS4 (circuit training) at Club Studio South Windsor

CS4 is a 50-minute circuit class that rotates through five stations: cardio intervals, strength training, functional exercises, and core work. It is designed for every fitness level, age, and goal. Callers may say circuit or circuit training.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## CS Strength at Club Studio South Windsor

CS Strength is form-focused dumbbell strength training held in the Box studio. It is a good choice for someone who wants to learn to lift with good technique. Class length is not published; plan on about 45 minutes.
Source: Club Studio schedule descriptions. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Ride (indoor cycling) at Club Studio South Windsor

Ride is a 45-minute, low-impact, rhythm-based indoor cycling class with lighting and music. The bikes accept SPD and Delta cleats, and regular sneakers are fine too. Callers may call it spin or cycling. Variations on the schedule: Strength Ride adds light dumbbell upper-body work; Party in the Club Ride and Rave Ride are themed rides whose exact format is not published.
Source: website FAQ and schedule. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Box classes (Box x Bags, Box x Lift) at Club Studio South Windsor

Box is a 45-minute, high-intensity, 9-round boxing class building boxing skills, cardio endurance, and strength. Boxing gloves are required and can be rented for $5; hand wraps are strongly recommended. Box x Bags is bag-based boxing. Box x Lift combines boxing rounds with heavy lifts and is better for people with some training experience.
Source: website FAQ and schedule descriptions. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Yoga classes at Club Studio South Windsor

Yoga runs in the heated Sweat+ studio. Yoga Flow is a 50-minute Vinyasa class in an infrared-heated room at about 90 to 95 degrees Fahrenheit. Yoga Restore is a warm restorative and Yin class with guided stretches for the shoulders, hips and spine, ending with meditation. Candlelight Restore is restorative yoga by candlelight. There is no yoga on Saturdays at South Windsor; Yoga Restore runs Sunday mornings and some weekday evenings.
Source: website FAQ and schedule. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Specialty classes at Club Studio South Windsor

Hot HIIT Sculpt is a heated, high-intensity sculpt class. Sound Bath Bliss + Stretch is a relaxing stretch and sound bath class, scheduled Sunday afternoons. Exact formats and lengths for these specialty classes are not published.
Source: South Windsor schedule. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Classes Club Studio offers elsewhere but not at South Windsor

Rebound, a trampoline cardio class, and Yoga Sculpt are brand-level formats that do not appear on the South Windsor schedule. Closest South Windsor alternatives: CS4 or Hot HIIT Sculpt for cardio, Pilates X Strength for toning.
