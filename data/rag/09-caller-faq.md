# Club Studio South Windsor - Common Caller Questions

Common questions phrased the way callers ask them. Facts as of November 6, 2026.

## Are you open right now? What are your hours at Club Studio South Windsor?

Monday to Thursday 5 AM to 10 PM, Friday 5 AM to 9 PM, and weekends 7 AM to 6 PM. Holiday hours aren't published, so for a holiday it's best to call the club at (860) 466-7628.

## Where is Club Studio South Windsor?

100 Cedar Avenue in South Windsor, Connecticut, at Evergreen Walk.

## How much is a membership at Club Studio South Windsor?

Online rates start at $169 a month for Standard. Standard Plus is $209 and adds priority booking and guest passes. Premier is $249 and works at every Club Studio. There's no initiation or annual fee shown online, and you pay your first and last month when you join. Tax isn't included.

## Is there a sign-up fee or contract at Club Studio?

The online rates show no initiation fee and no annual fee. Memberships are month-to-month and auto-renew. You pay the first and last month up front.

## Can I try Club Studio for free?

Yes. The website advertises a free pass, and the FAQ mentions a one-day guest trial. It doesn't say which applies to whom; the club can confirm.

## Are classes included in a Club Studio membership?

Yes. Memberships include boutique classes, up to five different classes a day, depending on space. You can reserve one spot per studio per day online.

## How far ahead can I book a Club Studio class?

Classes open a week in advance. Standard Plus and Premier members can book up to 10 days ahead.

## What if I need to cancel a class at Club Studio?

Cancel at least 12 hours before class and there's no fee. Later than that it's a $10 late-cancel fee, and a no-show is $15.

## What happens if a Club Studio class is full?

You can join the waitlist. If a spot opens you're added automatically and get a notification. Just remember late-cancel fees apply once you're in, so drop off the waitlist if your plans change.

## Do you have yoga on Saturday at Club Studio South Windsor?

There's no yoga on Saturdays. The closest options are Yoga Restore on Sunday at 9:15 AM, or Hot Pilates on Saturday at 8 AM.

## Do I need special shoes or equipment for Club Studio classes?

Reform needs grip socks. Box classes need boxing gloves, and you can rent them for $5. For Ride, sneakers are fine, or SPD or Delta cleats.

## How early should I arrive for my first Club Studio class?

About 15 minutes early for your first class, 10 minutes after that. Doors close when class starts.

## How hot are the heated classes at Club Studio?

Hot Pilates is up to about 85 degrees, Hot Pilates X Strength about 90, and Yoga Flow about 90 to 95. Yoga Restore is warm, not hot.

## Does Club Studio South Windsor have a pool, sauna, or steam room?

There's an indoor pool and a sauna. A steam room isn't listed for South Windsor.

## Does Club Studio South Windsor have pickleball or racquetball?

Neither is listed for South Windsor. Those are at select Club Studio locations only.

## Is there childcare at Club Studio South Windsor?

Childcare isn't listed for South Windsor.

## How old do you have to be to join Club Studio?

Sixteen, or thirteen with a legal guardian, who needs to sign for them.

## Can I bring a friend to Club Studio?

Standard Plus and Premier include 25 guest passes a year. Members can also send a friend a 7-day VIP guest pass from their online account.

## How do I cancel my Club Studio membership?

At the front desk, through your online account, or by mail.

## Can I freeze my Club Studio membership?

Yes, monthly members can freeze for $50 a month by contacting the club.

## Do you offer personal training at Club Studio South Windsor?

Yes. Pricing isn't published.

## Is there a cafe at Club Studio South Windsor?

Yes, Beaming Cafe, a juice bar.
