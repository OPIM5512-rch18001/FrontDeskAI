# Club Studio South Windsor - Choosing the Right Class

Groupings based on Club Studio's published class descriptions.

## Classes for complete beginners at Club Studio South Windsor

Good first classes: Reform (low impact, all levels), CS4 (designed for every fitness level), Ride (low impact, go at your own pace), and Yoga Restore (gentle and relaxing). Box x Lift and Hot HIIT Sculpt are more demanding and better after a few visits.

## Classes for relaxation and stretching at Club Studio South Windsor

For something relaxing: Yoga Restore (warm restorative and Yin yoga ending in meditation), Candlelight Restore (restorative yoga by candlelight, Monday evenings), and Sound Bath Bliss + Stretch (Sunday afternoons).

## Classes for cardio at Club Studio South Windsor

For cardio: Ride and its variations (Strength Ride, Party in the Club Ride, Rave Ride), CS4, Box x Bags, and Hot HIIT Sculpt. Ride is the lowest-impact cardio option.

## Classes for strength and toning at Club Studio South Windsor

For strength: CS Strength (form-focused dumbbell training), Pilates X Strength, Hot Pilates X Strength, Box x Lift, and Reform. CS Strength is the best fit for someone who wants to learn proper lifting form.

## Low-impact classes at Club Studio South Windsor

Gentle on the joints: Reform, Ride, Hot Pilates, Pilates X Strength, Yoga Restore, and Yoga Flow.

## Heated versus non-heated classes at Club Studio South Windsor

Heated (Sweat+ studio): Hot Pilates, Hot Pilates X Strength, Yoga Flow, Hot HIIT Sculpt; Yoga Restore is warm. Not heated: Reform, CS4, CS Strength, Ride classes, and Box classes.

## Classes by time of day at Club Studio South Windsor

Early risers: weekday classes start as early as 5:05 AM (CS4 and Reform). After work: weekday evenings run from 4:00 PM to about 7:45 PM. Weekends: Saturday classes run 7:15 AM to 11:00 AM; Sunday classes run 7:15 AM to 12:30 PM. Midday options are limited, mostly noon Reform or Pilates X Strength.

## Common ways callers name Club Studio classes

Callers often use other names. Spin or cycling means Ride. Reformer or the Pilates machine means Reform. Circuit means CS4. Hot yoga or Vinyasa means Yoga Flow. Restorative, Yin, or gentle yoga means Yoga Restore. Boxing means Box x Bags; there is no kickboxing class listed, so Box x Bags is the closest match. HIIT means Hot HIIT Sculpt or CS4. Weightlifting class means CS Strength. Mat Pilates means Hot Pilates or Pilates X Strength.
