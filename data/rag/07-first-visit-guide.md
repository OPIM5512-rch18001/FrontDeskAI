# Club Studio South Windsor - First Visit Guide

## What to expect on a first visit to Club Studio South Windsor

Arrive 15 minutes before a first class to check in at the front desk; doors close when class starts. Bring water and wear comfortable workout clothes. Locker rooms and towel service are available. For Reform, bring grip socks; for Box classes, bring or rent boxing gloves ($5) and wraps if you have them; for Ride, sneakers are fine. The club is at 100 Cedar Avenue in South Windsor, in the Evergreen Walk area.
Source: website FAQ and South Windsor club page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Heated classes for first-timers at Club Studio

Sweat+ classes are heated. Hot Pilates runs up to about 85 degrees Fahrenheit, Hot Pilates X Strength about 90, and Yoga Flow about 90 to 95. Yoga Restore is warm rather than hot. Reform, CS4, Ride, and Box classes are not heated.
