# Club Studio South Windsor - Cancelling or Freezing a Membership

These are the website FAQ's policies. A member's signed agreement takes priority over this summary.

## How to cancel a Club Studio membership

Most memberships are month-to-month and can be cancelled anytime in one of three ways: in person at the club front desk, through the member's online account, or by mailed written notice to P.O. Box 54170, Irvine, CA 92619. For mail, registered or certified mail is recommended, and keep a copy. Some states also allow cancellation by email, depending on where the member enrolled.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Cancellation timing and final billing at Club Studio

A mailed cancellation postmarked at least 5 business days before the next billing date should stop future charges. If it is postmarked later than that, one more charge may go through, and the FAQ says that charge is refunded. After cancelling, the prepaid last month is still usable; membership ends at the end of that prepaid month.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Cancelling a Club Studio membership with a minimum term

For agreements with an initial term of 3 months or more, cancelling during that term takes effect when the term ends, and cancelling early carries a termination fee. After the initial term ends, the normal month-to-month rules apply.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Freezing a Club Studio membership

Members on monthly dues can freeze their membership indefinitely for $50 per month per member. If the next billing date is within 10 days, the freeze starts with the following month's billing. Unfreezing mid-month means paying prorated regular dues for the rest of that month. To freeze, contact the club. Members can also ask about moving to another club instead of cancelling.
Source: website FAQ. Verified 2026-11-06.
