# Club Studio South Windsor - Guest Passes and Free Trials

## Trying Club Studio as a non-member

Club Studio advertises a few ways to try the club, and the website does not say which applies to whom:
- The website FAQ describes a one-day guest trial.
- The Free Pass page advertises a free 5-day pass that lets you book a class, and mentions picking up a complimentary guest pass in person at the club.
- Members can send a friend or family member a 7-day VIP guest pass through their online account.
The club can confirm which offer applies at (860) 466-7628.
Source: website FAQ, Free Pass page, member VIP Rewards page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Bringing a guest to Club Studio

Standard Plus and Premier memberships include 25 guest passes per year. Standard members can refer friends with a 7-day VIP guest pass from their online account. Club bookings cannot be transferred to a guest.
Source: rate page and member VIP Rewards page. Verified 2026-11-06.

## Club Studio referral reward

When a member refers someone who joins within 90 days, the member receives a $25 Club Studio retail credit, applied about 10 days after the new member enrolls. Short-term memberships are excluded and other restrictions apply. Rewards do not expire.
Source: member VIP Rewards page. Verified 2026-11-06.
